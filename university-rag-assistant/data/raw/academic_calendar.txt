Academic Calendar and Registration — Sample Dataset
Academic year: 2026–2027

Registration
Students should check the registration portal for the official add/drop dates each semester. Dates can differ between programs and may be updated by Academic Affairs.

Course withdrawal
Withdrawing from a course may affect degree progress and financial aid status. Students should consult the academic office before submitting a withdrawal request.

Advising
Students who are unsure whether a course satisfies a degree requirement should contact an academic advisor and bring their approved degree plan.
