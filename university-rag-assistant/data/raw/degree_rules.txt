Degree Rules — Sample Dataset
Academic year: 2026–2027
Owner: Academic Affairs

Minimum credits
Students must earn at least 120 credits to graduate from the undergraduate computing degree. Credit totals alone are not sufficient for graduation.

Required courses
Students must pass every course marked as required in their approved degree plan. A failed required course must be retaken and passed unless an official approved substitution or exception applies.

Prerequisites
A student may enroll in a course only after passing all listed prerequisite courses, unless the department has granted a documented exception. Being enrolled in a prerequisite at the same time does not count as having passed it.

Graduation review
Before graduation, the academic office verifies minimum credits, completion of required courses, and any program-specific conditions. The chatbot cannot make an official graduation eligibility decision.

Transfer credits
Transfer credits count toward the degree only after the university has evaluated and approved them. Students should not assume that a course taken elsewhere automatically counts.

Policy exceptions
Only written approval from the authorized department or academic office establishes an exception. Verbal statements do not replace documented approval.
